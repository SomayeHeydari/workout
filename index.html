What Is Pilates

Pilates can improve muscle tone, flexibility and strength

Pilates is a type of exercise that focuses on controlled and precise movements.

It can also help you become more aware of your body and movement.

Meal Plan

What to eat to stay in shape

How to eat without gaining weight ?

focus on balanced meals rather than skipping food or strict dieting.

  • Eat regular meals with vegetables/fruit + protein + whole grains or other carbs.

  • Include protein such as eggs, beans, yogurt, chicken, fish, or nuts.

  • Drink water regularly.

  • You can still eat sweets and snacks—just as part of an overall balanced diet.

  • Eat slowly and pay attention to when you're comfortably full.

  • Avoid crash diets, skipping meals, or severely restricting calories, especially while you're still growing.

A simple rule: eat a variety of nutritious foods, eat when you're hungry, and
don't try to keep your weight down by eating as little as possible.
